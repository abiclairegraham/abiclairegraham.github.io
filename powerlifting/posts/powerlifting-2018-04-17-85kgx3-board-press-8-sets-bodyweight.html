Powerlifting post

2018-04-17T21:06:31

2018

85kgx3 board-press (8 sets), bodyweight ~64kg. This is not the first time I've got a bench PB after training for 5 consecutive days in a row. Several of those recent days were *really* easy - like 5x5 on 60kg when my 5RM is more like 80kg. I have no doubt that these super-easy sessions are invaluable for building strength. Today's session was challenging but as you can see, still nowhere near max effort. This is about as tough a bench session as I ever do - and my bench progress the last 3-4 years has been very good indeed.
