Powerlifting post

2019-10-27T09:06:24

2019

Well, here I am with my friend @genopowerlifting yesterday having just become British Powerlifting M1 (ages 40-50) Champion in the 63kg class. A hard competition and a tough 5 weeks preparing since the Arnold European Classic last month, with various sleep issues, a nasty virus that I had to train through plus a bit of a knee imbalance that I've had to be very careful with and has meant I've been unable to do the occasional squat session - plus a very tiring week and terrible nights sleep before meant I felt pretty shattered even from the start of the comp! _ My plan going in was to try to build a big enough sub-total so that I could secure 1st place by the time of my second deadlift, meaning I could do a very risky 3rd deadlift attempt trying to break my own open British Record of 195.5kg set in March. _ Given how hard the comp was for me (taking a lot of determination because of how tired I felt) actually it was probably a good result that I did manage to get in that position. I deadlifted a very comfortable 182.5kg for my second lift giving me a 402.5kg total and guaranteed 1st place. I tried 196kg for my final lift but too heavy - just moved a few inches off the ground. _ So, nowhere close to any PBs on any lifts, but I achieved what I came to do and have hopefully ensured team selection for next year's IPF Classic Masters World Championships. _ Many thanks to my new friend Owen Smith for so generously offering to handle my attempts cards for me and voluntarily taking videos of my lifts, so kind and really helped me ð _ Thank you so much to the organisers of this fantastic competition Marc Giles and James Brincat Smith and all the volunteers - fabulous venue, so well-run, had the most sense of occasion of maybe any British comp I've ever been a part of. Congratulations and thank you to all. _ Lastly, thank you so very much to my dear Neil for his unending support, incredible patience and kindness through so many training sessions where I'm fed up and complaining!
