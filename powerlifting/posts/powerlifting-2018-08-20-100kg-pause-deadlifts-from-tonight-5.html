2018-08-20T21:28:26

2018

100kg pause deadlifts from tonight (54% 1rm). I added in pause deadlifts in the training cycle before the World Classic Masters in June, and I think they made a *big* difference to my deadlift. I had attempted 185kg at the European Masters Classic in March, and barely moved it a couple of inches off the ground - I successfully lifted this same weight quite comfortably in June. _ I don't think I ever actually noticed my hamstrings on an exercise before - but I feel them working really powerfully on pause deadlifts. _ I usually do 8 sets of 3 on this weight, and I do half the sets paused like this, and the other half as speed deadlifts - in both cases, I'm always trying to achieve maximum acceleration immediately off the floor and all the way to lockout. _ I also think in general that this easy second deadlift day helps my deadlift enormously compared to just doing one heavy deadlift session a week (even when it was just speed deadlifting and no pause-deadlifting). #ipf #girlswholift #ironladiesuk #britishpowerlifting #britishpowerliftingladies #britishpowerliftingwomen
