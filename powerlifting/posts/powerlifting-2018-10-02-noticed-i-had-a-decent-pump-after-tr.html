Noticed I had a decent pump after training tonight (did a lot of work in just one hour). I've trained 5 days a week for the last few years - apart from if it's Christmas or I'm on holiday in which case it's 3-4 times a week! _ Can't remember the last time I had a week off, but it was quite a few years ago. And I've trained in some shape or form for most of the last 18 years (I'm 40 years old now). Think I've added about 10kg muscle in those 18 years and lost a few kg of fat too. And I'm probably about 4-5 times stronger! _ I was a true weakling when I started, unable to do a single pressup until I'd trained for a few months.
