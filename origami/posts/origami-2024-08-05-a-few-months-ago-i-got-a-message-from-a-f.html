Curved

A few months ago, I got a message from a Facebook friend asking whether I did origami commissions, as he was looking for a gift to give his wife on their first wedding anniversary, traditionally the "paper anniversary". I hadn't actually done a commission before but after thinking carefully I decided to do it. I was a bit concerned about the responsibility of delivering something up to the occasion, but very touched that someone I'd never met trusted me for this (based on things I'd posted before on social media), and also, I hadn't done much origami for a couple of months and felt this could provide some new inspiration. My friend was very clear about the colour-palette that his wife would like, something minimalist, possibly even white.
