It's reverse-engineered from a picture I found (second photo), and I think the design is by @ekaterina.lukasheva ? Her work is amazing! Highly original, complex, very beautiful and mathematical. This flower ball is made from lots of small squares of paper, but no glue is used in the assembly. -The unit-piece is shown in the third picture - very simple. What's much more interesting is the rules for the construction/assembly. One of the most interesting models I've ever done. - - Each unit-piece has four "arms". Two of the arms are linked to *five* other units, but the other two arms link to only *three* other units. - -Assembly is definitely pretty fiddly as the units try to fall apart as more are added. But once the ball is complete, it's much more stable. - #origami#origamiart
