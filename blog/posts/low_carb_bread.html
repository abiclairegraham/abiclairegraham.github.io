My super-quick low-carb bread recipe

This protein bread is very low in carbs, and is free from many of the fermentable substances that cause people gut-problems, making it a very handy addition to various diets. Recipe inspired by a friend (Anastasia Zinchenko) and I eat it several times a week. It takes 10 minutes to make including cooking!!

It dries out very quickly indeed - but if you slice it and put it into a bag and freeze it, you can defrost it in the microwave when you need it and it will be as fresh as when it's newly made. The texture is very close to bread, it is very filling and a great protein-source.

This is the key ingredient: Vital refined gluten And this is my basic recipe:

Very low-card bread
1

Ingredients

Mix together in a microwavable bowl:

  • 1 scoop of gluten
  • 1/2 scoop of either pea-protein powder, faba-bean protein powder, soy protein powder or sunflower protein powder
  • seasoning (most basic would just be salt, and a pinch of some kind of sweetener just as a flavour enhancer. I find a little glucose powder works very well but use a non-caloric sweetener if you really want this low-carb.)
  • 1/2 scoop of powdered Flaxseed (optional but helps the texture)
  • 1 teaspoon of Psyllium powder (makes the dough more elastic)
  • 1 teaspoon of baking powder

2

Method

Now pour on just enough boiling water to make a bread dough sort of texture - not too wet and paste-like, but also, no dry ingredients left. You'll need to practice getting the amount of water right. Then, microwave for about 6 minutes. It will actually rise reasonably even with no raising agent - but if you use too much water it will not rise well and be a bit stodgy. That's essentially it - this won't have a crispy crust of course, but you can toast it if you fancy.

3

Some different flavour options

In terms of flavour, this really won't taste of much by itself. So, you need to add flavours - things like crispy onions, or sundried tomatoes. I sometimes add a little meat seasoning such as this to give just a little savoury flavour. Or you can make it sweet - add cinnamon, sweetener, nuts and raisins and eat with butter. Delicious 😀

4

Storage

You can freeze it very soon after cooking, and then defrost again in the microwave to make it good to eat. It will not have as nice a texture if you don't reheat it (apparently gluten starts to crystallize as bread goes stale, making it tough - but reheating causes the gluten to uncrystallise again and makes the bread nice and elastic again).

It can take a little experimentation to get it just right, but I hope you'll find this as useful as I have. I even use a variation of this to make low-card suet dumplings to put into meat stew, and also to make pizza-bases. Because it has so muhc protein in it, it's really filling - and, the gluten proteins contain a very complementary amino acid profile to both pea protein and collagen or beef protein-powder (typically made from connective tissue NOT muscle, so lacking critical amino acids). So it has some significant nutritional advantages also - for vegan diets, or anyone who wants some new ways to get more protein into their diet.
